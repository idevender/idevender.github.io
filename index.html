Bio

Hi! I am an undergraduate in the Honours Computer Science program at Memorial University of Newfoundland. I work on optimization for machine learning, asking how the symmetries of a model's loss constrain which solutions an optimizer can select and what that implies for generalization. In The Loss Does Not See the Basis, but Adam Does I turn one such symmetry into a criterion that predicts which optimizers inherit gradient descent's low-rank implicit bias and which structurally cannot. In earlier work with Tarun Sheel I introduced FlowAdam, an optimizer that injects momentum along a geometry-aware flow.

Before this work I was a research intern at Memorial's Visual and Analytic Computing Lab, advised by Matthew Hamilton and Vinicius Prado da Fonseca, who also supervised my Honours and Mitacs research, carried out in collaboration with Compusult Ltd. Earlier I held a Summer Undergraduate Research Award (SURA) at Memorial, working on light-field display simulation and rendering. On the industry side I have worked as a software developer at Verlo, an AI startup in St. John's.

I am applying to PhD programs. If my work is close to yours, I would genuinely like to hear from you — devenders@mun.ca.
